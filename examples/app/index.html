Pulse

Measures your heart rate and heart-rate variability using your phone's rear camera — no wearable needed.

Phone only for now.

Not a medical device. For personal curiosity only, not diagnosis or treatment.

This demo is built for a phone. Open ppg-js.vercel.app on your phone and put a fingertip over the rear camera.

Cover the lens and flash with your fingertip pad

Your phone's flashlight can't be turned on by this page (iOS Safari has no flashlight API). Turn it on yourself from Control Center, then cover the lens and the light with your fingertip.
